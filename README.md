### About Me

Final-year **Computer Systems Engineering** student at ESI-SBA. I am passionate about building systems where hardware, software, and AI work together. 

My work focuses on **Embedded Systems, IoT, and Edge AI**. I build complete data pipelines—from writing C++ firmware for ESP32 microcontrollers and integrating sensors, to training neural networks in Python and deploying them locally using TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers.

Alongside my engineering work, I am a UI/UX and graphic designer, which allows me to build complete, user-friendly interfaces for my systems. 🎨 See my design portfolio on [Behance](https://www.behance.net/fadlarekia).

I am currently seeking a **research internship** in Embedded Systems, Edge AI, or Applied Machine Learning starting in early 2027.
